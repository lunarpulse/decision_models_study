The fine-tuned Laya has now seen 12,000 labeled past claim decisions (trained on this very machine before the session). decider-4b stays untrained on purpose — it shows what a generalist can do with no data at all.

🚦 The ops floor — today's intake, decided in one pass

Every claim from the sealed 500-claim holdout, all four engines, measured once before the session. Pick an engine and set the auto-approve cutoff τ: a claim the engine rates at least τ likely to be fraud is held for the fraud unit; a covered, simple claim below τ is paid straight through; everything else goes to an adjuster. Override any claim — every counter follows you.
